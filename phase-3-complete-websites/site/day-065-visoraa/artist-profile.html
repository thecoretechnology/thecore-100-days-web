Artist profile / Painting · Berlin

Mara Voss:
the memory
of a surface.

Mara Voss works with translucent layers and interrupted forms, building paintings that feel like remembered places rather than fixed scenes.

↓
Biography

Between
presence and
absence.

Born in Hamburg in 1988, Voss studied painting before establishing a studio in Berlin. Her practice draws on architecture, faded photographs, and the shifting quality of daylight. She works slowly, allowing each layer to partially obscure the one before it.

Recent exhibitions include Soft Coordinates (2024) and Rooms Remember (2025).
