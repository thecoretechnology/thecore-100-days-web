Exhibition · Sep 18—Dec 08, 2026 · Main gallery

A softer
geometry.

Five artists explore how a line can hold a room, how a shape can become a feeling, and how balance can be beautifully unsettled.

↓
Curated by Lena Hart

Form is a
kind of language.

In A Softer Geometry, structure loosens. Painted fields, tactile objects, and precise interventions create an exhibition that shifts between certainty and intuition. The artists invite us to notice the quiet choreography of edges, shadows, and intervals.

Featuring Mara Voss, Theo Ren, Elise Moreau, Jonas Vale, and Inez Calder.
