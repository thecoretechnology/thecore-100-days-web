The Stillroom house

Come in,

Arriving

Ring the bell,
then come in.

There’s no reception desk and nobody behind glass. The front hall is where you’ll wait, and it’s about as calm as a hall can be.

Before you come
Nothing to fill in. Your therapist has read the notes from your call.

At the door
The door is on the latch during session hours. Ring once and come straight in.

In the hall
Hang your coat and take a seat on the bench by the window.

Afterwards
Sit in the garden or kitchen for a few minutes if you need to.
