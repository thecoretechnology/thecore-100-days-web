Both. Counselling tends to be shorter and focused on something specific; psychotherapy can go deeper and longer. On your free call we’ll suggest what fits, and you can change your mind later.

Some people come for six to eight sessions with a clear focus; others stay for a year or more. You review progress with your therapist every few weeks and decide together.

Some of our therapists are recognised by private medical insurers. Check what your policy covers, then mention it on your free call.

You don’t need to. Tell us what’s going on and we’ll suggest one or two people. If the first session doesn’t feel right, we’ll help you switch.

Everything stays between you and your therapist, who discusses their work anonymously in clinical supervision. The only exception is a serious risk to your safety or someone else’s.

We’re not an emergency service. If you’re in crisis, call NHS 24 on 111, Samaritans on 116 123 at any hour, or 999 if your life is at risk.
