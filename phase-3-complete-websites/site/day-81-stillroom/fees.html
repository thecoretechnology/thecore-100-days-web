In plain words

The small print.

Your therapist’s time and regular clinical supervision; a room in the house, tea in the kitchen and the garden between sessions; a review every six weeks; changing therapist if the fit isn’t right; and a receipt for every session.

By card after each session, in the house or by a secure link after online sessions. No packages, no deposits, nothing paid in advance.

Give us 48 hours’ notice and there’s no charge. With less notice the full fee is due, unless we can offer the time to someone else.

Some therapists are recognised by private medical insurers. Check your policy and mention it on your free call.
