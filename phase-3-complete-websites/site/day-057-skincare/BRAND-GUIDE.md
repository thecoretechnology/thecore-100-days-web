# AUREL Brand Guide

## Identity
AUREL is a thoughtful skincare concept built around considered everyday rituals. The primary signature combines a fine-line botanical bloom with an elegant serif AUREL wordmark and a widely tracked SKINCARE descriptor. This is the same logo lockup used on the product packshots and should remain consistent across every touchpoint.

## Logo usage
- Use `assets/logo/aurel-wordmark-dark.svg` on light backgrounds.
- Use `assets/logo/aurel-wordmark-light.svg` on dark/olive backgrounds.
- Use the icon variants for small square placements and favicon.
- Preserve the complete lockup proportions; do not recreate the mark with text or substitute the former geometric-A symbol.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the botanical emblem.

## Palette
- Warm ivory: `#F8F5EF`
- Soft cream: `#EEE8DE`
- Muted sage: `#A4A58B`
- Deep olive: `#444638`
- Charcoal: `#25251F`

## Typography
- Editorial headings: Playfair Display
- Interface and body: DM Sans

## Packaging direction
The six product packshots share a warm ivory base, deep olive accents, botanical AUREL emblem and consistent AUREL / SKINCARE typography. Keep these local packshots for every product tile, quick view, cart and hero composition.
