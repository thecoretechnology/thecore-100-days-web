✦ FAQS

Need more details?

What to know about this frontend product concept and its interactions.

What is Goldify?

Goldify is a Day 60 frontend concept for a focused gold-market analysis experience. The interface demonstrates dashboards, scenarios, alerts and integration patterns; it is not connected to live trading infrastructure.

Is the market data real-time?

No. All figures displayed in this demonstration are static sample data created to show the interface and interactions.

Does Goldify provide financial advice?

No. Nothing on this concept site is financial, investment or trading advice, and the website cannot place trades.

Can I try the interactions?

Yes. Explore the pricing switch, experience notes, responsive menu, 3D card tilt, FAQs and enquiry form.

Does the enquiry form send data?

No backend is connected. Submitting validates your fields and clearly explains that integration is still required.
