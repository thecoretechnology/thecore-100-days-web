The Still House

Residential · Chandigarh · 2026

THE BRIEF

A quieter kind
of everyday luxury.

Designed as a retreat from the pace of the city, The Still House is an exploration of softness, proportion and natural texture. A restrained palette lets light and material do the talking.

Warm oak, honed stone and tactile upholstery are layered with considered lighting and sculptural forms. Each space feels connected, calm and quietly individual.
